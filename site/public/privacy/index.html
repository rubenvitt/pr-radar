Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026 · applies to the PR Radar apps for macOS, iOS and iPadOS and to this website

In short

  • The apps do not send any data to us. There is no PR Radar server, no account, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your GitHub token stays on your device. The apps connect directly to GitHub and nowhere else. The only exception is checking a license key you bought directly (section 2.5).
  • We only receive personal data when you write to us or buy a license directly.

2. The apps

2.1 Nothing is sent to us

PR Radar runs entirely on your device. The apps do not send usage data, crash reports, device identifiers or content to us or to any analytics or advertising service. We have no access to your token, your repositories or your pull requests.

One exception comes from Apple: if you agreed on your iPhone or iPad to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides us with crash reports and usage statistics that do not identify you. You control this under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

iPhone and iPad: remove the token in the app and delete the app. That removes all of this data from the device. Existing device backups (iCloud or computer) may still contain the app's settings until they are replaced or deleted; you manage them in your device's backup settings.

Mac: delete the app and also the folders ~/Library/Application Support/pr-radar/ (settings) and ~/Library/Logs/PR Radar/ (log). If the app ever had to use the fallback log, it is in the folder pr-radar inside your temporary folder: run open "$TMPDIR/pr-radar" in Terminal and delete that folder. macOS also clears the temporary folder on its own. The Mac app does not store your token itself. You remove it where you keep it: unset GITHUB_TOKEN or GH_TOKEN, or sign out of the GitHub CLI with gh auth logout.

2.3 Connection to GitHub

To show pull requests, pipelines and releases and to perform actions such as merging, the apps connect directly to GitHub's servers:

  • api.github.com – queries and actions through the GitHub API, with your token
  • avatars.githubusercontent.com – profile pictures of authors
  • github.com – signing in with GitHub, and links you open in your browser
  • github.com and objects.githubusercontent.com – Mac only: checking for updates (once a day, if you allow it) and downloading them, without your token and without details about your system

For this, GitHub necessarily receives your IP address, your token and the requests. The provider is GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. GitHub processes this data as an independent controller under your agreement with GitHub, see GitHub's privacy statement. We receive none of it.

2.4 Notifications and background refresh

Notifications about red or green pipelines and merged pull requests are created by the app itself on your device. There is no push server. On iPhone and iPad the app refreshes in the background as often as iOS allows. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in the system settings.

2.5 Purchases and licenses

App Store: if you buy in the iPhone or iPad app, Apple is the seller and processes the payment under Apple's privacy policy. Apple does not give us data that identifies you.

Direct purchase (Mac): Mac licenses are sold by a reseller that acts as the seller towards you (merchant of record). It is named on the checkout page and on your invoice and processes payment and order data as an independent controller. From the reseller we receive your name, email address, country, order details and the license key. We use them to manage licenses, provide support and meet legal record-keeping obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR). We keep them for as long as the license exists and beyond that only as long as statutory retention periods require (up to ten years).

When you enter a license key in the app, the app sends it to the reseller's license API to verify it.

3. This website

This website is served through Cloudflare. The provider is Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit, Cloudflare processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, time, page requested and browser identifier to deliver the page and protect it from attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and stable website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details are in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising services and loads no fonts, scripts or content from third parties.

4. Contact by email

When you write to us, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR when it concerns a purchase or license, otherwise our legitimate interest in answering requests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We delete messages once the request is settled and no retention obligation applies. Our email provider processes messages on our behalf.

Please never send us your GitHub token. If you send us a log, check it first for anything you do not want to share.

5. Recipients

We do not sell data or pass it on for advertising. The only recipients are the service providers named above (reseller, hosting, email) and authorities where we are legally required to disclose data.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You can withdraw any consent at any time with effect for the future. An email to pr-radar@rubeen.dev is enough.

Right to object: where we process data based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member state where you live or work (Art. 77 GDPR).

7. Other

You are not obliged to provide us with any data. Without a token, however, the app cannot load data from GitHub. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

If the apps or the website change so that this policy is no longer accurate, we will update it. The version published here applies. In case of doubt, the German version prevails.
